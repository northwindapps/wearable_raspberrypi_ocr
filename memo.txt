--- InnoMaker IMX708 camera setup ---
1. Edit config:
   sudo nano /boot/firmware/config.txt
2. Change:
   camera_auto_detect=1
   to:
   camera_auto_detect=0
3. Add at the end of the file:
   dtoverlay=imx708
4. Save (Ctrl+X, Y, Enter), then reboot:
   sudo reboot
5. Verify camera is detected:
   libcamera-hello -t 0
   (or rpicam-hello -t 0 on newer OS)
